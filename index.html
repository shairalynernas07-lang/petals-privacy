Privacy Policy

Petal's De Lilio app. Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Petal's De Lilio collects through its mobile app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how we protect it. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, mobile number and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Order details: what you ordered, your delivery address, the map pin you place, your landmark and delivery notes, the date and time, and how you paid. For GCash or Maya payments we keep the reference number and any payment screenshot you send.
  • Messages: the messages and photos you send in the shop chat, and any rating or feedback you give.
  • Location: only when you choose to use it, for example "Use my current location" or dropping a delivery pin. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Delivery proof: a photo taken by the shop when your order is delivered, and the live location of the courier while the order is on its way.
  • Preferences: your delivery or pick-up choice, your favourite occasion, your light or dark appearance setting, and a notification token so we can send order updates.

2. Why we use it

  • To take, prepare, deliver and track your orders, to contact you about them, to answer your messages, to check payments, and to keep the app safe and working.
  • If you allow it, to send you news about new flowers and offers. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your orders.

3. Who can see it

  • Only the shop owner and the family members who run the shop can see your orders and messages.
  • We use trusted service providers to run the app: Google Firebase (accounts, database and notifications), Vercel (our server and photo storage), and OpenStreetMap-based map services (to show maps and look up addresses). They store data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Your payment itself happens inside GCash or Maya, under their own privacy policies.
  • We share information with the authorities only when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for business and tax records. You can delete a chat conversation yourself at any time; it is removed for you and the shop. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise the information we no longer need.

5. How we protect it

Your data travels over secure (encrypted) connections and is protected by security rules that only allow you to see your own orders and messages. If you turn on fingerprint sign-in, your sign-in details stay on your own phone and are never sent to us. The fingerprint itself is handled by your phone's system and is never seen by the app.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to its use, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to be compensated for damages. You can update most details yourself in My Account.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 9. If you feel we have not handled your data properly, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

7. Children

The app is meant for people 18 and older. Younger customers should place orders with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for major changes, tell you in the app.

9. Delete your account

To request that your account and data be deleted:

  1. Send an email to deliliopetals@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address on your account.
  2. We will delete your name, email address, mobile number, saved addresses and map pins, delivery notes, chat messages and photos, and profile photo, and remove your notification token.
  3. Order and payment records are kept only for as long as tax and business record rules require, and are then deleted. We will confirm by email once your account has been deleted.
